FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us

What's your design process like?
Our process includes discovery, strategy, concept development, refinement, and delivery. We work closely with you at every stage to ensure the final result exceeds expectations. Each project begins with a comprehensive consultation to understand your vision, followed by strategic planning, creative execution, and final delivery with full support.
How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary based on project scope. Logo design typically takes 1-2 weeks, while complete brand identity projects take 3-5 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our consultation to ensure alignment with your goals.
Do you accept payments in Naira?
Yes! We accept payments in both Nigerian Naira (₦) and US Dollars ($). For local clients, we prefer bank transfers in Naira. International clients can pay via wire transfer or PayPal.
Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Each package includes up to 2–3 revision rounds during the design phase to ensure the final result aligns with your vision.

Additional revisions can be requested at an agreed fee. Our goal is to create a brand identity that you love and that effectively communicates your message.
